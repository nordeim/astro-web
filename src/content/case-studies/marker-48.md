# Marker 48 Brewing

A coastal craft brewery brand with a story rooted in the Florida shoreline. We partnered with Marker 48 to build a brand system and digital presence that reflects their coastal heritage and craft-first brewing philosophy.

## The Challenge

Marker 48 had outgrown their original brand. As distribution expanded across Central Florida, they needed a more confident identity that could carry them onto retail shelves and into a broader craft-beer market — without losing the local character that made them popular in the first place.

## Our Approach

We started with a discovery sprint on-site at the brewery, interviewing the founders, the head brewer, and the front-of-house team. From there, we mapped the brand pillars — coastal, craft, community — and developed a strategic platform that informed every design decision, from the wordmark to the tap handles.

## The Solution

- **Brand identity** — Refined wordmark, color system anchored in coastal teal and weathered ink, and a typographic system that nods to nautical chart labeling
- **Website** — Custom-designed and developed with a focus on storytelling and the brewery's location-aware tap list
- **Print collateral** — Can labels, six-pack carriers, coasters, and event signage

## The Result

The rebrand launched alongside a new flagship IPA and was met with strong customer response. Distribution expanded into three new Florida counties within six months of launch.
