# Deals In Dirt

A bulk soil, mulch, and landscaping supply yard serving both landscape contractors and weekend homeowners. Deals In Dirt had a fifteen-year-old reputation and a brand that hadn't been touched since the founders hand-painted the original yard sign in 2009. The dissonance between the operation's actual sophistication (a 12-acre yard with a logistics software backend) and the brand's visual presentation (a clip-art shovel and a Comic-Sans-adjacent wordmark) was costing them contractor business.

## The Challenge

The yard's two customer types had opposite needs. Contractors wanted to log in, see real-time inventory, place bulk orders, and schedule deliveries without talking to anyone. Homeowners wanted to browse mulch varieties, see photos, and ask someone what they needed for a 200-square-foot flower bed. The existing site served neither — it was a single page with a phone number and a PDF price list that hadn't been updated since 2021. The brand, meanwhile, read as "mom-and-pop" in a category where contractors equate polish with reliability.

## Our Approach

We spent a week riding along on deliveries and shadowing the dispatch desk. The strategic insight was that the yard's actual competitive advantage was its logistics software — most competitors in the region still ran on paper tickets. So we made that the brand story: "Deals In Dirt" became the friendly front end of a sophisticated operation, not the other way around. The visual identity kept the founder's original logo concept (a shovel + a price tag) but redrew it from scratch in a confident, modern vocabulary. The site architecture split cleanly: contractor portal on one path, retail browse on the other, with the same product data feeding both.

## The Solution

- **Brand identity** — Redrawn wordmark and shovel-and-tag mark in a confident display sans; palette of weathered clay, forest mulch, and a single high-contrast ledger white for "the system"
- **Website** — Static marketing surface + authenticated contractor portal (built separately) sharing one product database; retail browse with photo-led product pages; contractor portal with bulk-order entry and delivery scheduling
- **Print collateral** — Yard signage system that doubles as wayfinding for delivery drivers; product spec sheets for contractors; homeowner-friendly "what you need for your project" reference cards

## The Result

The contractor portal alone eliminated an estimated 60% of inbound phone-order calls within the first two months. The brand refresh was well-received by both audiences — contractors reported the new identity made the yard "feel like a real supplier," and homeowners engaged more with the photo-led retail pages. The yard has since expanded into a second location.
