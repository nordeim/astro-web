# Croom Brewery

A rural Florida brewery brand built on heritage, hunting, and hard work. We partnered with Croom to develop a brand that honors the Croom tract of the Withlacoochee State Forest and the generations of hunters, fishers, and outdoorspeople who have called it home.

## The Challenge

Croom needed a brand that could stand out in a crowded craft-beer market while staying true to its rural Florida roots. The founders wanted to avoid the generic "outdoorsy" aesthetic that dominates the category and instead build something specific to place.

## Our Approach

We spent time in the Croom forest — on foot, on bike, and at the trailheads — to understand what makes the place distinctive. The brand strategy that emerged centered on three ideas: heritage (the land has a long human story), craft (the brewery is hands-on, not automated), and stewardship (we leave the forest better than we found it).

## The Solution

- **Brand identity** — Wordmark with a hand-cut feel, color palette pulled from longleaf pine and palmetto, custom illustrations of regional wildlife
- **Website** — Story-driven site with an integrated event calendar for trail cleanups and taproom happenings
- **Apparel** — Hats, tees, and hoodies designed for the trail as much as the taproom

## The Result

The brand resonated immediately with the local outdoor community. Taproom traffic doubled in the first quarter after launch, and the apparel line sold out its first run in three weeks.
