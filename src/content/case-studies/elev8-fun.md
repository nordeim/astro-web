# Elev8 Fun

A family entertainment center with three Florida locations, Elev8 Fun needed a website that could serve three distinct audiences: families booking birthday parties, teens looking for open-jump hours, and corporate event planners comparing group packages. Each audience wanted different information first — and each one's visit had to feel like the site was built specifically for them.

## The Challenge

The previous Elev8 site was a single long page that buried pricing three scrolls deep and routed every booking inquiry through a generic contact form. The operations team was fielding 40+ calls a day from people who couldn't find what they wanted online, and the corporate-events pipeline was almost entirely walk-in. On top of that, the brand had been patched together over four years of in-house graphic design — different logo variations in different places, no consistent type system, three different "primary" colors in active rotation.

## Our Approach

We spent two days on-site across the three locations — one weekday, one weekend — to see how the operations team actually used the website in conversations with customers. The insight that shaped everything: the booking flow was the product, not the marketing surface. So we built the site architecture around three role-based entry points (Families, Teens, Groups), each with its own pricing tier and booking path, and let the marketing content live one level deeper. For the brand, we did a full identity refresh — not a redesign from scratch, but a reconciliation: one logo, one type system, one primary color, with the playful secondary palette preserved.

## The Solution

- **Website** — Astro-built static site with three role-based homepages, each with its own booking CTA; per-location pages with real-time hours pulled from the operations calendar
- **Booking integration** — Custom booking widget that pre-fills party-package details based on the entry point (Families → birthday party flow; Groups → corporate-event intake form)
- **Brand system** — Single wordmark, single type pairing (Poppins for display, Inter for body), single primary color (a saturated aqua that reads as both "fun" and " trustworthy to corporate planners")
- **Marketing assets** — Email templates for the three audiences, printable birthday-party invitations, in-facility signage system

## The Result

The new site launched on a Tuesday. By the following Monday, the operations team reported a 30% drop in "where do I find pricing" calls, and the corporate-events pipeline received 11 qualified inquiries through the new intake form in the first week — more than the previous month combined. The brand reconciliation has been rolled out across all three locations' signage.
