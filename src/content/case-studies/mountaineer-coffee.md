# Mountaineer Coffee

A small-batch roaster operating at 4,200 feet, Mountaineer Coffee had outgrown its farmers-market roots and was ready to ship nationwide. The brand needed to carry both the romance of mountain elevation and the precision of a modern specialty roastery — without falling into the generic "rustic wood + kraft paper" aesthetic that dominates the category.

## The Challenge

The founders had been roasting on a 5kg batch roaster for three years and had built a loyal local following on the strength of single-origin Ethiopian and Colombian lots. The challenge was translating that local credibility into a national subscription business. The existing brand — a hand-drawn mountain illustration with a script wordmark — was charming in person at the farmers' market stall but read as amateur at ecommerce scale. The Shopify site was functional but visually indistinguishable from a thousand other small-batch coffee stores.

## Our Approach

We took a single-taste approach: the brand should feel like a well-edited field journal, not a souvenir. That meant disciplined typography (a single sans for everything, with weight — not face — doing the hierarchy work), a restrained palette (cold-pressed ink, paper, and a single warm wood tone for accents), and photography that focused on the process (roasting curves, elevation, the green-bean sourcing map) rather than the romantic landscape. The subscription experience was re-architected around a "roaster's pick" model — one curated bag per month, with the tasting notes and sourcing story told in the customer's voice, not the roaster's.

## The Solution

- **Brand identity** — Disciplined wordmark in a single high-contrast sans; "field journal" color system; sourcing-map cartography as the brand's signature visual element
- **Website & store** — Shopify build with a subscription-first information architecture; "roaster's pick" subscription flow with monthly tasting-notes storytelling; sourcing-map interactive on the About page
- **Packaging** — Bag labels that scale from 6oz sample to 2lb subscription; tasting-note cards that ship with every order, written in the second person

## The Result

Subscription sign-ups tripled in the first quarter after launch, with the "roaster's pick" tier driving 70% of new acquisitions. The sourcing-map interactive became the most-visited non-product page on the site, and the tasting-note cards are now collected and shared by subscribers on social — a wholly organic brand-extension moment the founders hadn't anticipated.
