# Unprofitable

A satirical card game brand whose entire premise is that everyone loses money. We partnered with the Unprofitable team to build an identity that could carry the joke across cards, packaging, and an online store — without the brand itself feeling like a throwaway gag.

## The Challenge

The founders are ex-startup operators who'd lived through three boom-and-bust cycles. They had the card mechanics, the writers, and the printing partner. What they didn't have was a visual language that could sustain the joke across expansion packs and merchandise without feeling one-note. They also needed an online store that could handle drop-ship printing and look like a real product brand — not a Kickstarter page.

## Our Approach

We started by reading every card in the deck. The humor is dry and specific (cap-table puns, term-sheet wordplay, an entire expansion dedicated to founder-CEO Pinterest boards), so the brand couldn't lean on generic startup iconography. We landed on a strategy of "anti-design as design": a deliberately conservative wordmark, a tongue-in-cheek corporate-blue palette, and a copywriting voice modeled on annual-report boilerplate. The store, by contrast, is bright and tactile — the punchline is that the corporate parent is drab and the product is alive.

## The Solution

- **Brand identity** — Wordmark set in a serious transitional serif; palette of corporate navy, off-white, and a single accent red reserved for the word "loss"; brand voice guidelines that read like a paralegal wrote them
- **Website & store** — Custom Shopify build with a two-mode toggle: "Corporate" (the brand-as-parent-company view, drab and procedural) and "Store" (the actual product, colorful and product-photography-led)
- **Print collateral** — Box art, expansion-pack sleeves, and a 16-page "Shareholder Report" mini-zine that ships with every first order

## The Result

The first print run sold out in eleven days, driven almost entirely by the Shareholder Report unboxing on social. The two-mode store toggle became a recurring mention in press coverage. The brand has since signed distribution with three indie-game retailers in the Southeast.
