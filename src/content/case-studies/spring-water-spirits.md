# Spring Water Spirits

A brand rooted in clarity and craft. We partnered with Spring Water Spirits to develop a complete brand system and digital presence that reflects their unique position in the market.

## The Challenge

The client needed a brand identity that would stand out in a crowded market while remaining authentic to their roots. They also required a website that could serve as both a marketing platform and a customer engagement tool.

## Our Approach

We started with a deep discovery phase — interviewing stakeholders, analyzing competitors, and mapping the customer journey. From there, we developed a strategic brand platform that informed every design decision.

## The Solution

- **Brand identity** — Logo, color system, typography, and brand voice guidelines
- **Website** — Custom-designed and developed with a focus on conversion
- **Marketing assets** — Print collateral, social media templates, and email campaigns

## The Result

The new brand launched to positive reception from both customers and industry peers. Website engagement metrics improved across the board, and the client reported a significant increase in qualified leads.
