# Beverlin Hills Quality Goods

A heritage goods brand with a digital storefront built for storytelling. We partnered with Beverlin Hills to build a brand and ecommerce experience that elevates everyday objects — leather goods, ceramic mugs, wool blankets — into items worth keeping for a lifetime.

## The Challenge

Beverlin Hills had a strong product line and a loyal local following, but their online presence was a generic Shopify template that didn't communicate the craft or the story behind each item. They needed a brand and a storefront that matched the quality of what they made.

## Our Approach

We started in the workshop, watching the team work leather and clay. The brand strategy that emerged centered on three ideas: provenance (every item has a maker and a material source), patina (these goods get better with age), and stewardship (buy less, keep it longer).

## The Solution

- **Brand identity** — Wordmark with a letterpress feel, warm color palette of nut-brown, cream, and forest green, custom product photography
- **Ecommerce** — Custom Shopify Plus theme with editorial-style product pages, integrated maker stories, and a "patina journal" where customers share how their goods age
- **Marketing** — Email and social campaigns built around the slow-craft narrative

## The Result

The new storefront launched ahead of the holiday season and conversion rate improved 64% over the previous template. Average order value grew 22% as customers engaged with the maker stories.
